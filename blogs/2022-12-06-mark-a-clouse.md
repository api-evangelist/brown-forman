          <h5>Mark is President of the NFL’s Washington Commanders, combining his passion for business and love of sports. Before his role with the Commanders, Mark spent more than two decades in the food industry and is the former President and Chief Executive Officer of Campbell Soup Company. Prior to that, he was CEO of Pinnacle Foods and held senior management roles at Mondelēz International, Inc. and Kraft Foods, Inc. Before joining Kraft, Mark served in the United States Army as a pilot and completed his service as a captain.</h5>

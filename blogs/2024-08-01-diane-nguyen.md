          <h3>Diane Nguyen is executive vice president, chief people and communications officer. Diane has served on the Executive Leadership Team since 2024.</h3>

<p>Diane most recently served as senior vice president, human resources director for our global commercial and corporate teams.<br /><br />
During her 16-year career at Brown‑Forman, Diane has served in a variety of roles providing human resource business partnership to Brown‑Forman’s businesses globally for commercial teams in North America, Europe, Emerging International, corporate functions, and previously, Global Supply Chain.<br /><br />
Before joining Brown‑Forman, Diane held human resource roles at Colgate-Palmolive, Homecare and Hospital Management, Aero Fulfillment Services, and United Bancorp of Kentucky.<br /><br />
Diane serves on the board of directors for Dare to Care in Louisville.<br /><br />
She earned her MBA from the University of Louisville and a bachelor’s degree in business administration from Eastern Kentucky University majoring in management and marketing.<br /><br />
Diane lives in Louisville with her husband and two children.</p>
